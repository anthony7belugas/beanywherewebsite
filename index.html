A few things to know.

What photos should I upload?

Use 4–6 clear photos of yourself, with different angles and good lighting. Use the same person in every photo, with no other people in the frame. BeAnywhere is for adults 18 and older.

Do I need an account or an app?

No signup or app download is required. Choose your shoot here, upload your selfies, and pay once. You can use your phone or computer.

How do I get back to my photos?

Open My photos in the same browser. We also email the finished photos as attachments, so you can save them on another device. Keep that email if you clear your browser data.

What does $3.99 include?

One AI photoshoot: 12 photos across six scenes, plus three reshoots. It’s a one-time payment in USD, with no subscription. AI results can vary.

Can I delete my photos?

Open My photos → Photo settings to remove your draft selfies or delete your cloud photos. Copies you downloaded or received by email remain with you. See our privacy policy for details.

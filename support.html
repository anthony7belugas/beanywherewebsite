My purchase or pack needs help

Open Your packs to check its status and use any retry or support option shown. If you’re still stuck, email us with your Support ID and the pack or purchase details.

Android: A pending Google Play payment must complete before your pack is available. Check the payment status in Google Play, then reopen BeAnywhere. If payment completed but your pack is missing, contact us for help.

How do I recover packs on a new phone?

iPhone: If you previously connected your packs to Apple, open Settings → Save & restore packs on your new Apple device and use the same Apple Account.

Android: If you previously connected your packs to Google, open Settings → Save & restore packs on your new Android device and use the same Google account.

Restored packs may take a few minutes to appear. Contact us if you need help finding your packs.

How do I request a refund?

iPhone: App Store refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com under Apple’s policies.

Android: See Google Play’s refund options or contact us for purchase support. Refunds are subject to applicable law and Google Play’s policies.

Contact us if you need help with a pack that did not finish.

Delete your BeAnywhere account and data

You can delete your BeAnywhere account and associated data in the app under Settings → Delete account.

You can also request deletion by emailing support@beanywhere.app, including if you no longer have the app. Tell us whether you used iPhone or Android and include your Support ID or linked account email if available. We may need to verify account ownership before completing your request.

To delete just your uploaded photos and generated packs in the app, choose Settings → Delete photos & packs.

Copies already saved to your device, shared, screenshotted, or delivered by email remain outside BeAnywhere. See our Privacy Policy for what is deleted and the limited records retained for accounting, security and related purposes.
